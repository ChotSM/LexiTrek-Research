LEXITREK RESEARCH REHEARSAL 2.1.0-quest-rehearsal.1

STATUS
This is a development/rehearsal release. It is not an expert-approved,
validated or school-device-verified main-study intervention. Use synthetic
codes and rehearsal records until all required approvals are complete.

OPEN THE APP
Publish these files at the repository root through GitHub Pages. Open the
HTTPS website. The default candidate pack contains 32 original single-word
records in four school-life blocks. It contains no copied textbook material.

QUICK REHEARSAL
1. Open Adventure. Choose an original explorer if desired.
2. Start the adventure, then choose any short map stop. In S1, each stop
   explores two words. Find the satchels and read the revealed word clues.
3. Try the crew labels, letter tiles, message sorting and route signs.
   Each stop also includes a short written form or meaning recall activity.
4. Try a wrong written response, a requested clue and a supported second try.
   A feedback-assisted response never counts as independent first success.
5. Collect a map piece, choose another stop, or Save & take a break.
   Pause within a stop, refresh and resume; your current work is preserved.
6. Save the final treasure. Observed instructional minutes remain unknown.
   Teacher > Open local teacher controls > Records and deviations > Record
   observed digital-lesson time can supply actual observations afterward.
   Teacher can also end a partial lesson from the pause screen.
7. Export the target register, local JSON record, response CSV and session CSV.
   Rehearse the ARCS observation and conventional delivery records.

Read docs/GAMEPLAY_REDESIGN.md for the changed task schedule and ARCS mapping.
The opening 24 lexical encounters are grouped into four two-word stops;
this is not a claim of reduced dosage or a validated stop duration.

CONTENT AND FREEZE
The 32 candidates are an independently authored development set, not a
verified KP2027/EVP list. Teacher/expert review and sense references are
pending. The final N may differ after the pilot; it must be settled before O1.
The app supports 4-120 single-word targets split across four nonempty blocks.
It does not imply that all resource-pool words should be taught or tested.

Export the draft pack. Independent reviewers should check the target senses,
curriculum links, ambiguity, English examples, BM meanings, accepted variants,
task burden and spelling policy. Record real review references in the pack.
Do not fill approval fields solely to unlock the software.

An approved pack needs:
- pack.status and pack.review.status set to approved;
- teacherReference, expertReference, protocolReference, rightsReference,
  deviceCheckReference, gameplayReviewReference and timingPilotReference
  filled with actual evidence identifiers;
- each word's teacher and expert review evidence;
- each word's EVP status checked, or not-applicable-with-reason, together
  with a sense-level reference or a documented reason;
- documented licences for any non-original material (none bundled here).

This release intentionally rejects imported audio paths because it cannot
verify a new audio package. It includes no audio playback or voice collection.
If pronunciation is teacher-provided, record it as delivered support.

Before O1, archive rehearsal records, clear that rehearsal run, import the
reviewed pack, then freeze the research run. The freeze stores version,
SHA-256 fingerprint, time, N and evidence references. It checks required
fields; it cannot independently certify the truth of a review or an approval.
The proposal, assessment booklet/key and institutional correspondence must
remain in restricted research storage, not this public source repository.

EIGHT LESSONS
Each lesson is planned as 30 minutes: about 5 for goals/review, 20 for
vocabulary activities, and 5 for independent class work and closure.
S1 introduces A. S2 reviews A and introduces B. S3 reviews A/B and introduces
C. S4 reviews B/C and introduces D. S5 emphasises A/B and selected C/D.
S6 emphasises C/D and selected A/B. S7 interleaves meanings and sentence gaps.
S8 supplies final cumulative form and meaning recall practice.

The actual task counts are shown in Teacher and vary with N. No claim is
made that the candidate load fits 20 minutes for all learners. Rehearse it.
Earlier blocks have more planned review opportunities; do not claim equal
exposures across words. A displayed card is logged as an acknowledgement,
not proof of reading, understanding or successful recall.

RECALL AND SUPPORT
Revealed clues teach words with original contexts and BM support.
Recognition and letter building are supported practice. Introductory stops
alternate typed form recall and typed meaning recall. Later cumulative
sessions practise both directions. A meaning response can be in English or BM.
Optional pre-response clues are logged as support. Leaving a written response
blank records that it was not recalled. Feedback then gives the connection;
one written retry is available and is always logged as feedback-supported.
A word reveal is a display record, not proof of reading or understanding.

Meaning responses matching a draft accepted wording receive practice
feedback. Other nonblank explanations are marked review_pending, not
automatically wrong: a human must consider valid paraphrases. This is not
the primary-test marking system. Final scoring policies need separate review.

INDEPENDENT ASSESSMENTS
Record only administration dates in this app. Administer tests externally.
O1: before S1; proposed 3-7 school days beforehand.
O2: 24-48 hours after S8.
O3: 28 calendar days after O2; document actual day and days 26-30 window.
Form recall comes first and its response sheets are collected before meaning
recall displays target spellings. No hints, word banks, app access or answer
feedback are supplied during tests. Do not return marked answers before O3.

No primary outcome scores, formal items or scoring keys are stored here.
For N approved words, the proposed full external test has 2N responses.
Its length, allowed meanings/spellings and equal-weight composite need
expert/pilot justification. Absent tests are missing, not automatic zeros.

PRACTICE PAUSE AFTER S8
Ending S8, including a partial S8, changes this device to assessment_hold.
Word-bank access and target-word lessons are blocked. Recording O2 changes
the phase to follow_up without reopening content. Recording O3 does not
silently start extra practice. A separate rehearsal can be started only by
the teacher after archiving completed-run records.

These are client-side behavioural controls. Downloaded JavaScript/data can
be inspected or copied by a technically able user; this is not DRM, secure
remote authentication or prevention of every incidental exposure. A real
study needs school-managed devices, controlled offline copies, no assigned
target-word home review, and a deviation log. Browser date/time is not a
trusted study clock. Verify actual timing externally.

LOCAL RECORDS AND CONFIDENTIALITY
Use codes such as P001 and C1, never names, IDs, emails or PBD histories.
A research browser run belongs to one coded learner. Other learners require
separate managed profiles/runs; do not overwrite one learner's records.
Rehearsal records are not research data. A pupil without research recording
authorisation can still learn; item response events are not retained in
research mode for that pupil. This checkbox records an operator's decision
under the actual approved consent process; it does not obtain consent.

The system stores records locally and does not upload them. Browser storage
is not encrypted and local teacher controls are not access authentication.
Use the institution's approved restricted devices and export destination.
Automatic central synchronisation, accounts and remote monitoring are not
implemented. Records must never be committed to a public repository.

Response exports include unique event ID, release fingerprint, coded learner,
class, lesson, quest, interaction, task type, response, support, first attempt
and outcome. Grouped item response times remain null; a separate quest
activity event records elapsed time for the group. Do not invent item times.
Map pieces and badges reward completed practice, not primary-test mastery.
Sessions distinguish 30 planned minutes, observed instructional minutes,
technical interruption minutes, task-active time, and partial completion.
Browser task-active time is an implementation estimate, not observed teaching
time or a measure of attention. Hidden tabs are paused automatically.

The research merger rejects rehearsal files, version/pack mismatches,
non-authorised records and conflicting duplicate IDs. Identical repeated
exports are deduplicated. It combines learner event/session ledgers only;
keep the original exports, observer records, conventional logs and assessment
administration records separately under the approved management procedure.

ARCS CHECKLIST
The eight observer indicators are based on the researcher's supplied
Appendix A. Rate 0, 1, 2 or NA and supply observed evidence. These ratings
are design/fidelity documentation, not an IMMS or pupil motivation outcome.

COMPARISON CONDITION
Teacher documentation covers the same approved targets, scheduled time and
external assessments. Record normal active conventional teaching without
pretending that it replicates the digital quest mechanics or exact exposure
counts. Do not display LexiTrek practice to the comparison class before O3.

OFFLINE AND UPDATES
Wait for Ready offline after opening the hosted website online on each device.
Then reload offline and check tasks, data, writing, feedback and saved state.
Caching uses the same source pack and engine online/offline. Browser data
clearance/eviction removes local records and resources; export routinely.
Old v1 pilot caches at the same scope are removed. Earlier localStorage is not
imported into this version, and earlier records are not automatically deleted.
A download of an earlier v2 local run is offered in Teacher when present.
If the earlier run was frozen for research, this redesign blocks its own
pupil content on that browser. Preserve that run and review the new game in
a separate development browser profile; do not reopen study practice during
the retention interval by switching versions.
The service worker is cache-first for a stable rehearsal release. A future
update must bump the cache and application version together. The new quest version also uses a separate local storage key. Version changes
alter the fingerprint and prevent silent continuation of a frozen run.

BEFORE MAIN-STUDY USE
Complete teacher/expert/pilot and rights reviews, device and offline testing,
institutional permissions, actual class allocation and teacher arrangements,
independent test review, protocol reconciliation, and a release freeze.
No built feature certifies an ethical approval, effective intervention,
validated test, or causal treatment effect.
