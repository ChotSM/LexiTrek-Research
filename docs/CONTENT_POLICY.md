# Content and intellectual property policy

This release retains the user's project name LexiTrek. All bundled characters, SVGs, instructions, word explanations, BM explanations, contexts and practice prompts were authored for this prototype. They are not copied textbook pages, extracts, recordings, dictionary entries or DELIMa resources. This provenance statement is not a guarantee of universal legal clearance or a determination of institutional ownership.

The candidate words are ordinary English single-word lexical targets organised independently into four school-life themes. The complete source glossary and its sequence have not been reproduced. Curriculum and sense-level reviewers must provide their own verified references before a research freeze; no protected definition needs to be copied into the public data.

## Release rules

1. Include independently authored material or assets with documented permission for the exact web/offline uses intended.
2. Keep the creator, source, licence/permission evidence, modifications and attribution obligations in the asset record.
3. A new recording of a protected script is not treated as an original script. New listening materials need independently written language and appropriate performer rights.
4. Do not include textbook passages, exercises, emails, poems, illustrations, layouts, scans, source-wording fields, DELIMa links/integrations or third-party portal access.
5. Do not include commercial character likenesses. The ten explorer portraits are original simple SVG constructions.
6. Adding AI output, a citation, an educational purpose or a private-repository setting is not an automatic copyright clearance.
7. Check any future code, fonts, audio and media licences before bundling. This build has no external fonts, analytics, runtime packages or remote media calls.
8. Do not publish participant records, PBD information, the submitted proposal, formal test booklets, formal answer keys or institutional correspondence.
